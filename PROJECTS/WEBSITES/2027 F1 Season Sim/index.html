2027 Formula 1
season simulator

The 24-race 2027 calendar with the 2026 drivers and teams. Each weekend runs session by session, from practice and the three qualifying segments to a race you can watch lap by lap with pit stops, crashes and safety cars, or you can let the whole year run.

The calendar is the FIA schedule announced on 16 September 2026, and the drivers and teams are the 2026 grid. Track outlines are hand-drawn from each circuit's known layout rather than surveyed data, so corners are approximate. Team pace ratings are my own estimates from 2026 form, and every result is random, so treat the outcomes as entertainment rather than predictions.
