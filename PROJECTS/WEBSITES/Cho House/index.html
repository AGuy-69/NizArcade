Cho’s House

Keep Cho happy, clean and fed
Feeling okay
💛 Morale
🫧 Cleanliness
🍎 Hunger
How to play
  • Drag Cho onto a room above to take him there.
  • Toss him gently and he lands on his feet for bonus morale. Fast throws, high drops and wall slams hurt his morale and cleanliness.
  • Drag food to his mouth, toss toys at him, scrub him with soap.
  • Press the shower knob, flip the bedroom lamp to sleep, switch on the TV.
  • Click things in each room: close the curtains, switch lamps and string lights, run the sprinkler, flush the toilet, change the TV channel, wake the bird.
